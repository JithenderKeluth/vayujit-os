# Product gap analysis

The central gap is not missing screens; it is missing trustworthy business artifacts across the first journey. A seller can create a goal and inspect technical research state, but cannot yet reliably see a real, identifiable product with source evidence and proceed to a human selection.

Primary gaps:

1. Live fetch configuration blocks the first useful artifact.
2. Budget-language parsing loses `₹` and produces an unresolved capital constraint.
3. Fixture/local states span many supplier, intelligence and AI surfaces; provenance must stay explicit.
4. Product comparison, selection, sourcing, economics and decision stages are structurally present but not proven end-to-end on real evidence.
5. Production deployment dependencies (credentials, approved domains, workers, backups, alerting and rollback) are external to the local implementation.

The closure strategy is vertical and evidence-first, not a rewrite of existing modules.
