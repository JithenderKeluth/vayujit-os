# Financial readiness checklist

- [ ] Unit acquisition, packaging, shipping, marketplace fee, tax, and selling
      price assumptions have an owner and evidence source.
- [ ] Currency is explicit (initially INR); no silent conversion is used.
- [ ] Contribution and break-even calculations identify estimates versus actual
      marketplace settlement events.
- [ ] Missing inputs remain UNKNOWN and produce an Action Center signal.
- [ ] Settlement reconciliation, refunds, and fees are provider-sourced or
      clearly marked synthetic; no bank-verified claim is made without bank
      evidence.
- [ ] A HUMAN reviews the commercial decision before any launch authorization.
