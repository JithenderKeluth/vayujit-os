# First-seller business onboarding guide

The authenticated Launch readiness screen (`/operations/launch-readiness`)
composes existing owner-scoped settings, catalog, inventory, marketplace,
financial, backup, health, and Action Center authorities. It does not create a
duplicate business or owner record.

Complete the following in order and leave anything not evidenced as UNKNOWN or
BLOCKED:

1. Owner identity and business contact in Settings; confirm Asia/Kolkata and
   INR operating assumptions where applicable.
2. Seller channels and categories in the canonical catalog/marketplace
   workspaces. A completed form is not proof of a marketplace account,
   trademark, GST registration, or legal approval.
3. Product, variant, internal SKU convention, images, supplier evidence, costs,
   shipping assumptions, category, and listing draft. Review missing fields.
4. Inventory location, unit of measure, stock evidence, reorder threshold, and
   HUMAN confirmation of physical opening stock.
5. Financial assumptions and fee evidence. Unknown values must remain UNKNOWN;
   estimates are not settlements or bank-verified events.
6. Marketplace account readiness, permissions, and unmapped SKU review. Keep
   synthetic/read-only transports until separate provider authorization.
7. Backup/recovery verification and Action Center review.

The Launch readiness page links each area to its evidence workspace and shows
`NOT STARTED`, `IN PROGRESS`, `READY FOR REVIEW`, `BLOCKED`, or `VERIFIED`.
Business Agent and Action Center are advisory. Final go-live always requires an
explicit HUMAN decision outside this projection.
