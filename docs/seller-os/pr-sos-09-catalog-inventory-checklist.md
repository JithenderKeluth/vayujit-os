# Catalog and inventory launch checklist

## Catalog

- [ ] Product identity and category are owner-reviewed.
- [ ] At least one variant has a unique internal SKU.
- [ ] Images are private, durable, safe, and have approved alt text.
- [ ] Supplier association and evidence provenance are visible; no retail page
      is treated as wholesale proof.
- [ ] Acquisition, packaging, shipping, fee, tax, and selling-price inputs are
      evidence-backed or explicitly UNKNOWN.
- [ ] Marketplace category and listing content pass canonical readiness.
- [ ] Listing draft is HUMAN reviewed; publication remains disabled.

## Inventory

- [ ] Location, unit, on-hand, reserved, damaged, available-to-promise, and
      reorder threshold are recorded in the canonical inventory authority.
- [ ] Opening stock has evidence and an explicit HUMAN confirmation.
- [ ] Channel SKU mappings are unique, owner-scoped, and unmapped identifiers
      remain visible.
- [ ] Provider observations are not converted into physical stock.
- [ ] Reconciliation exceptions and low-stock actions are reviewed in Action
      Center before launch.
